About

Eighteen years of shipping products people hold in their hands.

I am a senior mobile and frontend engineer and mentor. I have spent my career helping startups and established businesses design, develop, and launch applications — and I have also led teams as a fullstack engineer and CTO.

The work has taken me across the USA, Canada, Switzerland, Germany, and New Zealand: native iOS in Objective-C and Swift, React Native and Flutter products, and the web stacks around them. Firebase, AWS, MongoDB, and GraphQL show up often.

Outside engineering I hold a Level 5 TEFL certification. Volunteering and translation have always been part of the path. I care about language, mentoring, and making cross-cultural collaboration feel ordinary.
