01 About

Eighteen years of shipping products people hold in their hands.

I am a senior mobile and frontend engineer and mentor, working from Switzerland with teams around the world. I have spent my career helping startups and established businesses design, develop, and launch applications, and I have also led teams as a fullstack engineer and CTO.

The work has been with companies around the world: native iOS in Objective-C and Swift, React Native and Flutter products, and the web stacks around them. Firebase, AWS, MongoDB, and GraphQL show up often.

Outside engineering I hold a Level 5 TEFL certification. Volunteering and translation have always been part of the path. I care about language, mentoring, and making cross cultural collaboration feel ordinary.
